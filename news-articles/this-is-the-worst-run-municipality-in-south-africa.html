This is the worst-run municipality in South Africa

August 8, 2026 | OpenMicFM News

Maluti-a-Phofung Local Municipality in the Free State has been named the worst-run municipality in South Africa.

The assessment is reported in the FW de Klerk Foundation Local Government Report Card. The report places renewed attention on municipal performance, accountability and the delivery of services to residents.
