Womens DAY Commemorate

August 8, 2026 | OpenMicFM News

Only one day remains before the community gathers at Earnest Swanepoel Hall in Dispatch for a special Women’s Day commemoration.

The event brings local residents together to recognise the strength, contribution and achievements of women in the community. OpenMicFM will continue sharing updates and community stories from the event.
